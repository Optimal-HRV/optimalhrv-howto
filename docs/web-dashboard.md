# Optimal HRV Web Dashboard

The Optimal HRV Web Dashboard at [app.optimalhrv.com](https://app.optimalhrv.com) is where coaches, clinicians, and managers bring people into an organization, organize them into groups, follow their HRV and biofeedback practice, and manage billing.

> **Note:** This guide covers the **Professional** workspace, which has the organization and group tools. Your own readings and sessions are in the **Personal** workspace, which isn't covered here.

---

## 🚀 1: Getting Started

### Sign In

1. Go to [app.optimalhrv.com](https://app.optimalhrv.com).
2. Sign in with **Google**, **Apple**, or your **email and password**. Use the same account you use in the Optimal HRV mobile app.
3. If you forget your password, click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

> **Tip:** Tick **Remember me** on a computer you trust so you stay signed in.

### Switch to the Professional Workspace

Every dashboard account has two workspaces. You can switch between them with the toggle at the bottom of the left sidebar:

- **Personal:** Your own readings, biofeedback, mindfulness, and RF assessments.
- **Professional:** The tools for your organization and groups. Everything in this guide is here.

Click **Professional** to open the organization dashboard. The label above the toggle shows your current role, for example _Workspace · Organization admin_.

### Get Set Up Checklist

When you first open the Professional workspace, a **Get Optimal HRV set up** card at the top of the dashboard walks you through five steps:

1. **Take the dashboard tour**
2. **Create your first group**
3. **Add members with a QR code or link**
4. **Approve anyone waiting to join**
5. **Open one member's page**

Click **All steps** to see the list, then click the button next to any step to jump straight to it. You can replay the tours whenever you like from the **Help and tours** (?) button in the top bar.

### Find Your Way Around

**Left sidebar (Professional workspace):**

| Menu item | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Organization overview: practice summary, member list, group health, and who needs a check-in |
| **Manage groups** | Create, open, edit, and delete groups |
| **Manage members** | Search, add, edit, and remove members |
| **Add member** | Shortcut that opens the **Add members** panel from any page |
| **Join Requests** | Approve or decline people who joined with a QR code or join link |
| **Reports & Analytics** | Readings and biofeedback analytics by group or member, plus data export |
| **Connections** | The organizations you belong to, your role in each, and **Create organization** |
| **Notifications** | Membership, health, inactivity, and clinical alerts |
| **Invoices** | Your organization's invoices: pay them or download PDFs |
| **Billing** | Rate, active members, estimated charge, payment cards, and billing email |
| **Settings** | Your profile, sign-in, and display preferences |

**Top bar:**

- **Organization context** (next to the back arrow): Shows which organization you're working in and your role. Click it to switch organizations (see [Switch Organizations](#switch-organizations)).
- **Search** (⌘K / Ctrl+K): Find members, organizations, and groups quickly.
- **Help and tours** (?): Replay the guided tours.
- **Notifications** (bell), **Dark mode** toggle, and your **account menu**.

### Roles and What They Can Do

When you add someone or edit their role, you choose one of these roles:

| Role | What they can do | Can see health data? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Owns the organization and has full access, including giving people admin-level roles | Yes |
| **Organization admin** | Manages all members, groups, billing, and settings in the organization | Yes |
| **Organization manager** | Manages people, groups, and billing | **No** |
| **Group admin** | Manages their group and can view its members' health data | Yes (own group) |
| **Group manager** | Manages the members of their group | **No** |
| **Member** | Takes readings and practices in the app. Has no admin tools. | Only their own |

> **Note:** You can only give someone a role below your own. For example, an Organization admin can't make another person an Organization admin. If you need to give someone a role at your level, ask your organization **Owner**. The form shows this reminder as _"Need a peer role for someone? Ask your organization owner."_

## 👥 2: Adding Members

**Who can do this:** Owners, Organization admins and managers, Group admins and managers (for their own groups)

To add members, open **Manage members** and click **Add members** (top right), or click **Add member** in the sidebar. The **Add members** panel offers three ways to add people:

<div align=center>
<img width="420" src="./images/web-dashboard/new/add-members-options.png" alt="Add members options"/>
</div>

| Option | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Share a QR code or join link** ⭐ _Recommended_ | Adding several people at once. They scan or open the link and ask to join. |
| **Create their account** | When the account needs to be ready right away. You set the name and a temporary password. |
| **Add someone already in your organization** | Giving an existing member a new role or adding them to another group. No invite email is sent. |

### Option 1: Share a QR Code or Join Link (Recommended)

This is the fastest way to add a class, team, or client list. You create a code, share it, and people request to join on their own.

**How joining works:**

- **Approval is on by default.** People wait in **Join Requests** until you approve them.
- **You choose who pays:** your organization, or each member.
- **Codes never expire** unless you set an expiration date.
- **You can revoke a code at any time.** People who already joined stay in the organization.

<div align=center>
<img width="420" src="./images/web-dashboard/new/join-link-panel.png" alt="Join link and QR code panel"/>
</div>

#### Create a Join Link / QR Code

1. Go to **Manage members** → **Add members** → **Share a QR code or join link**.
2. Click **Generate QR** (or **Generate QR Code** if you haven't created any yet).
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Label (optional):** A name to help you recognize the code later, for example _"Front desk flyer"_.
   - **Groups \*:** Pick one or more groups. Each group gets its own join link and QR code with the same settings.
   - **Who pays for the subscription?** Choose **Organization pays** or **Member pays**. You can change this later from the member's profile.
   - **Require approval to join:** When this is **on**, people wait for you to approve them. When it's **off**, they join right away.
   - **Custom expiration:** Leave it **off** so the code never expires, or turn it on and choose an end date.
4. Click **Generate QR Code**.

<div align=center>
<img width="420" src="./images/web-dashboard/new/generate-qr-code.png" alt="Generate QR Code form"/>
</div>

> **⚠️ Important:** Anyone can share or forward a QR code. If your **organization pays**, keep **Require approval to join** turned on. Otherwise anyone who gets the code can join and be billed to your organization.

#### Share and Manage Your Codes

Every code you create is listed in the **Share a QR code or join link** panel. For each code you can:

- **Copy** the join link to send by email, text, or chat
- **Print** or display the QR code, for example on a flyer, a slide, or the front desk
- **Revoke** the code when you no longer need it

Use the **All Groups** filter to see the codes for one group, and switch between the list and grid views with the icons next to it.

#### Approve Join Requests

When someone scans your QR code or opens your join link, their request appears on **Join Requests** (when approval is required).

1. Open **Join Requests** from the sidebar.
2. Check the **Pending** count at the top. For each request, the table shows the **Requester**, **Group**, **Source**, **Payer**, **Message**, **Reason**, **Status**, and **Submitted** date.
3. **Approve** the people you recognize and **Decline** requests that don't belong.

Use the **All Status**, **All Sources**, and **All Groups** filters to narrow the list. You'll also get a **Membership** alert on the **Notifications** page when someone is waiting.

<img width="800" src="./images/web-dashboard/new/join-requests.png" alt="Join Requests page"/>

### Option 2: Create Their Account

Use this when you want the account to be ready immediately, for example to set someone up during an appointment.

1. Go to **Manage members** → **Add members** → **Create their account**.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **First name \*** and **Last name \***
   - **Email \*:** This is the address they'll use to sign in.
   - **Temporary password \*:** A secure password is filled in for you. Use the 👁 icon to show it, the **copy** button to copy it, or the **refresh** button to generate a new one.
   - **Gender** (optional)
   - **Role \*:** Usually **Member**. See [Roles and What They Can Do](#roles-and-what-they-can-do).
   - **Group(s) \*:** One or more groups the person will belong to.
   - **Who pays for the subscription?** **Organization pays** or **Member pays**.
3. Click **Create account**.

<div align=center>
<img width="420" src="./images/web-dashboard/new/create-account.png" alt="Create their account form"/>
</div>

#### After Creating the Account

1. **Share the login details securely.** Send the person their email address and temporary password.
2. **Ask them to:**
   - Install the Optimal HRV app ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/optimal-hrv/id1501292206) / [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.optimalhrv.app&hl=en_US&gl=US)) and sign in.
   - Change their password, and fill in their date of birth and gender in their profile. These are used to calculate population norms.

> **Already has an account?** If the email is already registered with Optimal HRV, you can't create a new account for it. Send that person a **QR code or join link** instead so they can join with their existing account.

### Option 3: Add Someone Already in Your Organization

Use this to give an existing member a new role (for example, making them a Group admin) or to add them to another group. No new invite email is sent.

1. Go to **Manage members** → **Add members** → **Add someone already in your organization**.
2. **Person \*:** Search for and select the member.
3. **Role \*:** Choose their role.
4. **Group(s) \*:** Choose the groups to add them to.
5. Click **Add member**.

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 3: Managing Members

### The Manage Members Page

**Manage members** lists everyone in the current organization. It has these columns:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Member** | Name and email. Click it to open the member's profile. |
| **Last Active** | When they last used the app |

| **Health** | Their recent HRV (RMSSD) and trend compared with their own average, for example _On track_, _Improving_, _Inactive_, or _Never active_ |
| **Status** | Whether the account is active |
| **Actions** | The **⋯** menu: **View Profile**, **Edit**, **Remove from organization** |

**To find people:**

- Type in **Search by name or email…**
- Filter by group (**All Groups**), by activity date (**Last active**), or by role (**All Roles**)
- Click a column header to sort by it
- Change how many people you see per page with **Rows** at the bottom

### Edit a Member

1. On **Manage members**, click **⋯** at the end of the member's row → **Edit**.
2. The **Edit Member** panel has three tabs:
   - **Basic:** First name, last name, email, phone, and date of birth.
   - **Assign:** Group(s), role, and **Who pays for the subscription?** (Organization pays / Member pays)
   - **Prof.:** Professional details (title, credentials, specialization, bio). You edit these on the member's profile page, not in this panel.
3. Click **Next** to go between the tabs, then click **Save Changes**.

<img width="420" src="./images/web-dashboard/new/edit-member-assign.png" alt="Edit Member, Assign tab"/>

### Move a Member to Another Group or Change Their Role

You can do this in either of two ways:

- **Edit** the member (see above) and change **Group(s)** or **Role** on the **Assign** tab, **or**
- Use **Add members** → **Add someone already in your organization**.

> **Tip:** A member only shows up in a group's dashboards and reports once they've been added to that group.

### Change Who Pays for a Member

1. Click **⋯** → **Edit** → **Assign** tab.
2. Under **Who pays for the subscription?**, choose **Organization pays** or **Member pays**.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

Members set to **Organization pays** are billed to your organization (see [Billing](#💳-6-billing-and-invoices)).

### Remove a Member

1. Click **⋯** at the end of the member's row → **Remove from organization**.
2. Confirm.

They're removed from your organization and its groups, so they no longer appear in its dashboards and reports.

### View a Member's Profile

Click a member's name, or **⋯** → **View Profile**. The profile shows:

- **Header:** Name, roles, status, email, total counts (readings, biofeedback, mindfulness, RF assessments), last sign-in, and last activity
- **Tabs:**
  - **Readings:** Morning and anytime HRV readings and their trends
  - **Biofeedback:** Optimal Zone summary, key metrics (Optimal Zone, Training Efficiency, Low Frequency, Max–Min), time in each zone, session length mix, practice streak, and **Session history**
  - **Mindfulness:** Mindfulness sessions
  - **RF assessment:** Resonance frequency assessment results
  - **Profile:** Personal and professional details
- **Filters:** Change the date range (for example **Last 30 days**) and click **Refresh**.
- **Export:** Download the member's RRI data (see [Export Data](#export-data)).

## 📈 5: Following Your Members' Progress

### Organization Dashboard

**Dashboard** (Professional workspace) shows your whole organization at a glance:

- **View:** Switch between **Readings** (HRV readings) and **Biofeedback** (biofeedback and mindfulness practice).
- **Filters:** Choose a date range (for example **Last 30 days**) and session types (**Biofeedback**, **Mindfulness**), then click **Refresh**.
- **Practice summary:** A short summary of the period in plain language, for example _"Quiet week for the organization — 2 of 56 members were active"_. Expand **What's this based on?** to see the data behind it.
- **Key metrics:** Optimal Zone time (latest, average, and best) in the Biofeedback view, or RMSSD in the Readings view.
- **All members:** Each member's trend compared with their own usual, their streak, last activity, and status (**On Track**, **Improving**, **Inactive**, **Never Active**). Click **View →** to open a member's profile.
- **How each group is doing:** One card per group with its average and how many members are active. Click a card to filter the page to that group.
- **Who's been active:** How many members opened the app each week.

### Member Follow-Up: Who Needs a Check-In

The **Member follow-up** section on the dashboard, on group pages, and in Reports shows who may need your attention this week:

| Bucket | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Below their usual** | Still practicing, but their Optimal Zone time this period is more than 15% below their usual. The biggest drops are listed first. |
| **Gone quiet** | Practiced before, then stopped in the last 30 days. The most recent stops are listed first. |
| **Long quiet** | No practice for 30 days or more. These people aren't counted in _Need check-in_. |
| **Never started** | Joined or were invited, but haven't had a session yet. |

Click **Show list** on a bucket to see everyone in it.

<div align=center>
<img width="800" src="./images/web-dashboard/new/member-follow-up.png" alt="Member follow-up"/>
</div>

### Reports & Analytics

**Reports & Analytics** is the deeper view, for the whole organization, a group, or a single person.

1. **Choose a view:** **Readings** or **Biofeedback**.
2. **Narrow the scope:** Use **Groups** and **Members** to focus on specific groups or people.
3. **Set filters:**
   - **Date range:** for example **Last 30 days**
   - **Readings view:** **Morning**, **Anytime**, and **Tags**
   - **Biofeedback view:** **Biofeedback** and **Mindfulness**
4. Review the results:
   - **Readings:** Group average RMSSD, members improving (RMSSD above their own average), members active, and members who need a check-in
   - **Biofeedback:** Group average Optimal Zone time, total sessions, members who practiced, and members who need a check-in
   - Member list, member follow-up, and group cards, as on the Dashboard

<div align=center>
<img width="800" src="./images/web-dashboard/new/reports.png" alt="Reports & Analytics"/>
</div>

#### Key Metrics Explained

- **Optimal Zone:** The share of a biofeedback session spent in the heart rhythm that shows effective HRV training. Sessions are split into **Optimal Zone**, **Medium**, and **Low**.
- **RMSSD:** Root Mean Square of Successive Differences, the main time-domain HRV measure used in readings.
- **Training Efficiency, Low Frequency, Max–Min:** Other biofeedback metrics you can switch to in **Key metrics**.

### Export Data

You can download raw **RRI (RR interval) data** for analysis in any HRV tool (for example Kubios, R, or Python).

- **For a whole organization, group, or filtered list:** On **Reports & Analytics**, click **Export**. This exports everyone currently in scope (up to 50 members).
- **For one member:** On the member's profile, click **Export**.
- **For your own data:** Go to **Settings** → **Account** → **Export my data**.

**Steps:**

1. Under **What to export**, choose the activities: **Readings**, **Biofeedback**, **Mindfulness**, and/or **RF assessments**.
2. Choose a **Date range**.
3. Check the summary (who is included, activity types, and the date range in UTC), then click **Start export**.

<div align=center>
<img width="420" src="./images/web-dashboard/new/export-rri.png" alt="Export RRI data dialog"/>
</div>

**What you get:** A ZIP file with one folder per member and one subfolder per activity:

```
hrv-rri-export/
  manifest.json
  {MemberName}_{userId}/
    morning/
    anytime/
    biofeedback/
      2026-08-01_10-15-AM_S12345.csv
    mindfulness/
    rf-assessment/
```

- Each CSV has a single **RR** column, with one value per heartbeat in milliseconds.
- File names use UTC times and include the reading ID (`R…`) or session ID (`S…`).
- Activities without RRI data are listed as skipped in `manifest.json`.

## 💳 6: Billing and Invoices

**Who can do this:** Owners, Organization admins, and Organization managers. Group admins and managers can't see billing.

### How Billing Works

Organizations are billed **$4.99 per active member per month**. There are no seat packs or prepaid tiers. Your **Billing** page always lists exactly which members are counted in the current period. Members set to **Member pays** pay for their own subscription.

### The Billing Page

Open **Billing** from the sidebar to see:

- **Rate:** $4.99 per active member per month
- **Active members:** How many members count toward the current billing period, and the period's dates
- **Estimated charge:** For example _3 × $4.99 = $14.97_
- **Billing day:** The day of the month invoices are created, and the date of the next invoice
- **Billing email:** Where receipts and invoices are sent. Click **Change** to update it.
- **Active members this period:** Exactly who is being billed for, with their name, email, and a **View** link to their profile. No health data is shown here.

<div align=center>
<img width="800" src="./images/web-dashboard/new/billing.png" alt="Billing page"/>
</div>

### Add a Payment Card

1. On **Billing**, under **Payment methods**, click **Add card** (or **Add your first card**).
2. Enter the card details and save.

With a card on file, invoices are paid automatically. Without one, you can pay each invoice yourself from the **Invoices** page.

### Invoices

Open **Invoices** from the sidebar to see your organization's billing history:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Invoice** | Invoice number |
| **Date** | Invoice date |
| **Amount** | Total billed |
| **Status** | For example **Pending** or **Paid** |
| **Action** | **Pay** an open invoice, or download it as a **PDF** |

Use **All Status** and **Date range** to filter the list.

## 🏢 7: Organizations

### Switch Organizations

If you belong to more than one organization:

- Click the **Organization context** button in the top bar and choose an organization, **or**
- Go to **Connections** and click **Switch** next to the organization you want.

The organization you're currently using is marked **Using now**. Every page, including Dashboard, members, groups, reports, and billing, shows data for that organization only.

### Connections

**Connections** lists every organization you belong to and your role in each. For each one you can:

- **Switch:** Make it your current organization.
- **Manage:** Open its organization page.
- **Groups:** Jump to its groups.

<div align=center>
<img width="800" src="./images/web-dashboard/new/connections.png" alt="Connections page"/>
</div>

### Create an Organization

1. Go to **Connections**.
2. Click **Create organization** and fill in the details.

You become the **Owner** of the new organization. If you need help setting up an organization for your team or clinic, email `support@optimalhrv.com`.

### Organization Page and Settings

Open it from **Connections** → **Manage**, or from **Settings** → **Manage organization**. It has these tabs:

- **Overview:** Organization info (name, type, email, phone, website, owner), billing details, and address. Click **Edit** to update them.
- **Groups**, **Users**, **Billing**, **Invoices:** The same tools as the sidebar pages, for this organization.
- **Settings:**
  - **Allow join requests:** Lets people ask to join (for example with a QR code).
  - **Allow self-signup:** Lets people join without an invitation.
  - **Require approval:** An admin must approve new members.

## ⚙️ 8: Your Settings and Notifications

### Settings

Open **Settings** from the sidebar:

- **Profile:** Photo, name, phone, date of birth, gender, country, title (Dr., PT, RN, Coach, and so on), and degrees or certifications. Click **Save changes** when you're done.
- **Account:**
  - **Send password reset email:** Get a secure link to choose a new password. Your email address comes from your sign-in provider and can't be changed here.
  - **Refresh app data:** Clears cached charts and data on this device and loads a fresh copy. You stay signed in.
  - **Export my data:** Download your own readings and sessions as a ZIP file.
- **Preferences:** **Dark mode**, **Soft avatar colors**, and **Compact sidebar**.

### Notifications

**Notifications** collects the alerts for the organization or group you're currently viewing:

- **Categories:** **Membership** (join requests and invites), **Health**, **Inactivity**, **Clinical**
- **Status filters:** All, Unread, Read
- **Mark all read** to clear the list

Invites sent to you by other organizations also appear here.

## 🔧 10: Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **I can't see Manage groups, members, or Billing** | Switch to the **Professional** workspace with the toggle at the bottom of the sidebar. If it's still missing, your role may not include that tool (see [Roles](#roles-and-what-they-can-do)). |
| **I'm looking at the wrong organization's data** | Use the **Organization context** button in the top bar, or **Connections** → **Switch**. |
| **"Email already exists" when creating an account** | That person already has an Optimal HRV account. Send them a **QR code or join link** instead. |
| **Someone scanned the QR code but isn't in my organization** | Check **Join Requests**. Approval is on by default, so they're waiting for you to approve them. |
| **A member doesn't appear in a group's reports** | **⋯** → **Edit** → **Assign** tab: make sure the group is selected, then save. |
| **A member shows "Never active" or no data** | They haven't taken a reading or done a session yet. Ask them to sign in to the mobile app with the same email and record a session. |
| **I can't give someone the Organization admin role** | Only someone at a higher level, usually the organization **Owner**, can give a role at your own level. |
| **The billing count looks wrong** | On **Billing**, check **Active members this period** and each member's **Who pays** setting (**⋯** → **Edit** → **Assign**). |
| **Charts look stale** | Click **Refresh** on the page, or go to **Settings** → **Account** → **Refresh app data**. |
